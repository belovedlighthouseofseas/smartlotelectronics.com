What it does well

A boring hub, in the best possible way.

We've been running the Aurora Hub Pro across three homes since January. Forty-seven devices on it total — Hue bulbs, Aqara sensors, two Yale locks, a handful of Ikea plugs, plus a Tuya kettle we threw at it for fun. Nothing has dropped off in 112 days of testing.

The thing that surprised us most: it stayed online during a 36-hour internet outage. Scenes ran. Motion-triggered lights still triggered. The app obviously couldn't reach us, but inside the house everything kept working.

What it doesn't

The catches we found.

  • ↳ The iOS app is unremarkable. You'll spend more time in Home Assistant or Apple Home.
  • ↳ Initial Zigbee pairing for very old devices (pre-2019) can take two or three tries.
  • ↳ Thread border-router behavior with mixed-vendor networks is good but not flawless. If you have a Nest Hub on the same Thread mesh, expect occasional rediscovery.
  • ↳ Not Wi-Fi 6E — Wi-Fi 5 only. Doesn't matter for a hub, but the spec sheet implies otherwise.
From our test log
After 112 days we kept three of the four hubs we tested for ourselves. The fourth went to a friend's parents and they haven't called us once — which, for smart home gear, is the highest compliment.
