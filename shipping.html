Shipping

Fast 2-day US standard, free over $35. We ship same-day on orders placed before 2pm Pacific, Monday–Friday.

US shipping

  • Standard 2-day: $4.99 — FREE on orders $35+
  • Express overnight: $14.99
  • Bulk / pallet: custom quote — contact bulk@

Standard orders typically arrive 2–4 business days from order date. Express orders placed before 2pm PT ship same day and arrive next business day.

Carriers

We use UPS, USPS, and FedEx depending on destination and package size. You'll receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships. Track it any time on the order tracker.

International

Currently shipping to US and Canada only. UK and EU launching fall 2026. International customers may be responsible for customs duties and import taxes.

Order confirmations

You'll get three emails per order: confirmation, shipped (with tracking), and delivered (with photo confirmation when available).

Issues

Lost in transit, damaged on arrival, or missing your bonus item? Email hello@ within 14 days and we'll make it right.
