Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026. The short version: we collect what we need to ship your order and improve the store. We don't sell your data.

What we collect

When you place an order: name, shipping address, billing address, email, and phone (if provided). When you browse: standard analytics (page views, device type, referrer). We use cookies for cart persistence and basic analytics — no third-party advertising trackers.

What we do with it

  • Fulfill and ship your order
  • Send order updates and tracking
  • Handle returns and warranty claims
  • Improve product selection and the store experience
  • Send marketing emails — only if you opt in, never sold to third parties

What we don't do

We don't sell your data. We don't run third-party retargeting pixels on this site. We don't share your contact info with anyone outside the small set of vendors required to fulfill your order (shipping carrier, payment processor, email provider).

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to delete it, or opt out of marketing emails at any time. Email privacy@smartlotelectronics.com.

Cookies

We use a single first-party cookie for your shopping cart and basic site analytics. No third-party advertising or social pixels are loaded by this site.

Updates

If this policy changes materially, we'll notify subscribers by email and post the update date at the top of this page.
