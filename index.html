How a lot gets listed

Four steps between auction and your door.

STEP 01

Source

We buy from manufacturer overstock, returns lots, and pre-release test units — never gray-market imports or pulled-listing inventory.

STEP 02

Stress-test

Every unit gets a power cycle, firmware audit, and benchmarks for the spec we'd lie about if we were the manufacturer. About 18% of what we buy never gets listed.

STEP 03

Live-in

The remaining units go into one of seven test homes for ninety days — Wi-Fi 6 mesh, plaster walls, kids, pets, the works. Failures get logged, not edited out.

STEP 04

List honestly

If we kept one for ourselves, we say so. If we'd buy it again, we say so. If there's a catch — a missing app feature, a flaky integration — that goes on the product page in plain English.
