How it works

It's the simplest reward system in tech retail. There are no points, no membership fees, no codes to enter, no email opt-ins required. Add items to your cart, and as your cart hits 1, 2, and 3+ items, the bonus tier upgrades automatically. The bonus item ships free in your box.

What "Bonus Eligible" means

Every product on Smart Lot is bonus eligible. The label is there to remind you that the item counts toward your reward tier — there's nothing to do, it's already on.

What "Bundle & Save" means

Some products are paired into pre-built bundles for an extra discount on top of the bonus. A 2-item bundle saves you up to $10 AND unlocks the Tier 2 premium bonus. A 3-item bundle saves you up to $30 AND unlocks the Tier 3 VIP pack. See our Bundle Deals page for live combos.

Can I pick my bonus?

Yes — at checkout you'll see the bonus selector for your tier. Pick your favorite from the rotating bonus catalog. Bonus catalogs refresh weekly, same time as new product drops.

Do bundles count as multiple items?

Yes. A 2-item bundle counts as 2 toward your tier, a 3-item bundle as 3. So a single bundle can unlock the next tier on its own.

The honest take

The bonus program exists because the average tech buyer leaves the page after one item. We'd rather throw in a real bonus than spend that money on retargeting ads. You get more, we keep more new-customer trust. Both sides win.
