Common questions

Before you write us.

What's the actual return process?

Email returns@ with your order number. We send a prepaid label within an hour during business hours. Refund hits your card 2-4 days after the package scans in. No restocking fee. No "you opened the box" deductions.

Do you ship internationally?

US and Canada today. UK and EU coming this fall. If you're outside that and want a quote on a freight pallet, email bulk@ and we'll work something out.

Can I get a per-unit manifest before I commit?

Yes, for any lot above $500. Email bulk@ with the SKU and we'll send the burn-in log, condition grades, and original retail per item — usually within a few hours.

Do you take affiliate or sponsored placements?

No. Not ever. The catalog is what we'd buy ourselves and the order reflects that. If a brand offers, we politely decline.
